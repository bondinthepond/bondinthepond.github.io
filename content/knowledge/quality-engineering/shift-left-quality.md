## Overview

Shift-left is not simply testing earlier. It is designing delivery practices so quality feedback arrives before defects become expensive.

## Benefits

- Reduced QA bottlenecks
- Faster feedback
- Better engineering ownership
- Earlier validation of APIs, queues, databases, and integrations
