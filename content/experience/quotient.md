## Highlights

- Built and led a 12-member Quality Engineering team supporting Coupons.com, Brandcaster, Coupon Codes, National Rebates, and related B2B/B2C platforms serving large-scale consumer and business use cases.
- Led development and quality teams across the complete SDLC, including planning, design discussions, development coordination, test strategy, release readiness, production validation, and continuous improvement.
- Drove automation transformation from manual-heavy testing toward automation-first quality engineering across UI, API, mobile, backend, and performance testing layers.
- Played a critical role in bringing Coupon Codes ownership in-house from a vendor-led model, including codebase ownership, delivery practices, test processes, automation frameworks, deployment pipelines, environments, and integration testing.
- Supported cloud modernization and infrastructure consolidation initiatives across Azure, AWS, and on-prem environments, contributing to improved scalability, reliability, and operational efficiency.
- Promoted shift-left quality practices by enabling early validation through API testing, backend validation, test data management, architecture reviews, and developer-QE collaboration.

## Areas of Impact

### Engineering Leadership

- Built, managed, and mentored a 12-member scrum team across developers, QA engineers, automation, backend validation, performance testing, and release support.
- Led engineering execution across development and quality responsibilities, balancing code delivery, testing depth, release readiness, and production quality.
- Defined hiring expectations, interview practices, onboarding approaches, growth plans, and performance management processes for engineers and QA team members.
- Created a culture of ownership, engineering discipline, collaboration, and continuous improvement across the scrum team.
- Coached engineers and QAs to move beyond task execution toward system understanding, automation design, architecture awareness, and shared quality ownership.

### SDLC Ownership

- Led cross-functional engineering delivery across planning, development, testing, release, and post-release validation for multiple consumer and business-facing platforms.
- Managed quality engineering responsibilities and broader SDLC execution for application teams across product, engineering, QA, program management, and infrastructure stakeholders.
- Established delivery discipline through structured planning, sprint execution, release readiness reviews, defect triage, and continuous improvement practices.
- Partnered with product managers, engineering leaders, designers, and project managers to align quality strategy with roadmap priorities, business outcomes, and customer experience.

### Automation Transformation

- Transformed testing strategy from predominantly manual testing to automation-first quality engineering across web, backend, mobile, and performance testing.
- Built and scaled automation frameworks using Selenium, REST Assured, Cypress, Appium, PyTest, TestNG, Java, Python, and supporting CI/CD tools.
- Improved automation reliability by stabilizing locators, optimizing wait strategies, improving framework structure, reducing flaky tests, and strengthening test data handling.
- Integrated automation suites into CI/CD workflows to improve regression execution, release confidence, and feedback speed.

### Platform Modernization

- Contributed to the architectural design and modernization of the Coupon Codes platform using Java Spring Boot, React, microservices, and distributed system principles.
- Supported the transition of Coupon Codes from vendor-led delivery to internally owned engineering, codebase, and quality practices.
- Participated in application design, backend workflow understanding, integration strategy, API validation, and system-level quality planning.
- Helped modernize legacy systems and improve maintainability, scalability, and delivery ownership across product teams.

### Shift-left Quality

- Introduced shift-left practices by involving QE earlier in requirements, design discussions, architecture reviews, and testability planning.
- Enabled earlier validation through API-first testing, backend checks, test data automation, integration testing, and environment readiness practices.
- Helped reduce dependency on final UI readiness by validating services, APIs, data flows, and backend behavior earlier in the sprint cycle.
- Supported shorter delivery cycles and improved feedback loops through structured sprint execution and early quality involvement.

### Cloud & Release Excellence

- Supported cloud migration and infrastructure consolidation initiatives across Azure, AWS, and on-prem environments.
- Defined validation strategies, release readiness checks, production sanity practices, and rollback risk assessment for critical application releases.
- Used monitoring and observability tools such as Splunk and Pingdom to support production validation, incident analysis, and release confidence.
- Led performance, SEO, and customer-experience quality initiatives using JMeter, Locust, Lighthouse, and related tools to identify bottlenecks and improve business outcomes.
